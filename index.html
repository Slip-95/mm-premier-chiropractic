Hi, I'm Dr. Mistie Ahrens

I opened M&M Premier Chiropractic with one mission: to help you live your best life, free from pain. I believe in the beautiful “ripple effect” of chiropractic care — when you leave feeling amazing, that positivity spreads to everyone around you.

I’m passionate about offering a natural, non-invasive alternative to pills and surgery. There’s a time and place for everything, but surgery should be the last resort, not the first step.

After learning that only about 40% of chiropractors participate in Medicare, I made it my mission to welcome Medicare patients with open arms. Everyone deserves access to quality care.
